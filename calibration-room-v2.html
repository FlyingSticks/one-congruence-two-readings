How the room fits the seam

The dial

Perspective's single center Zp is split, by construction, into two lines at two depths: Zv = Zp − D/2 (vertical slit, governs x) and Zh = Zp + D/2 (horizontal slit, governs y). The vertical slit scales x by Zv/(Zv − z), the horizontal scales y by Zh/(Zh − z). Their ratio is the aspect a frontal square casts,

AR(z) = (Zv/Zh) · (z − Zh)/(z − Zv),

a Möbius map of depth, and it is exactly the cross-ratio (Zv, Zh ; screen, z). At D = 0 the two factors coincide, AR ≡ 1 at every depth, and the single center can no longer tell one depth from another — that is what makes a pinhole a pinhole.

The neutral surface and the three means

The depth where the two magnifications exactly cancel — equal in size, opposite in sign — is the harmonic mean of the slit depths, z* = 2ZvZh/(Zv + Zh) = Zp − D²/4Zp. There AR passes through −1: the four canonical points of every ray sit in harmonic position. The gold chip in the scene is that surface. At D = 0 it sits on the eye; as the dial opens it slides forward toward the screen, but only to second order in D. Splitting one center yields three, ordered as the classical inequality of means: the harmonic mean is the neutral surface, the geometric mean √(ZvZh) is the mirror radius of the six-step duality check (small gold tick on the axis), and the arithmetic mean is the old station point.

Two readings of one congruence

The rays through the two slits are one congruence of lines. Read as rays collapsed to their pierce-points on the screen, it is the cross-slit camera: transversal frames stay rectangles, depth lines bow toward the central vanishing point (hyperbolic arcs; the Bowing Theorem). Read as edges — each vertex a graduated projector with depth laid off linearly from footprint toward the slit — it is glide: depth lines straight, x graduated by (Zv − z)/Zv and y by (Zh − z)/Zh, reaching the image axes at the slit depths. Per channel the two gauges are reciprocal, m·g = 1 along every ray, so glide's aspect is 1/AR: the glide is the cross-slit reflected through the mirror of the neutral geometry, and both readings sit at −1 on the neutral surface. The two coincide at D = 0 and split exactly where the two slits are genuinely at two depths.

Reading, correcting, budgeting

Read. Three fixed marks on every ray (the two slits and the nominated screen) plus the object make four, and four points on a line have a cross-ratio. Invert the Möbius map and the frame's aspect is its own depth: the panel closes this loop live, decode(AR) against the room's true working depth. Correct. At the working depth z₀ the anisotropy is one affine stretch, the same everywhere on the sheet, so one reciprocal factor 1/AR(z₀) squares it exactly — the ▢ button. Through a depth extent Δ the residual after squaring is εx = D·Δ / 2(z₀ − Zv)(z₀ − Zh) to first order (A13.3); at the rig constants that is about four units of clean band per percent. The bow the factor cannot reach is O(Δ²). Budget. The scene belongs to whichever projection its proportions make cheaper: Δ·D against B². The presets carry the essay's cases — the wide section (glide's home ground), the corridor forced through the dial (perspective, and the plate's numbers: sagitta 1.5 on a 44.3 chord, AR saturating toward Zv/Zh = 0.6).

Rig: screen z = 0, Zp = 200, D = 100 → Zv = 150, Zh = 250, z* = 187.5. Verified in check_calibration_room_v2.js — 32 checks pass, run against the rig math this page embeds, including the draft's plate figures.

Turn the dial from zero: the slits part, the frames stretch as the aspect cue engages, the gold chip leaves the eye, and the two readings split. Slide z₀ and watch the decoded depth stay locked to the true one. Widen Δ and the bow grows while the correction's residual runs past tolerance; widen B and the budget turns toward glide. Press ▢ to square the frame at z₀ with one factor — the neutral surface's harmonic balance carried to the working plane.
